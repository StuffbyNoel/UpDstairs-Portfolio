THE CONCEPT

UpDstairs is an immersive virtual reality experience centered on spatial presence, physical hand interactions, and vertical exploration. Designed from the ground up for VR headsets, every mechanic prioritizes comfort and tactile feedback.

Engineering the project involved optimizing 3D environments for smooth high-framerate VR rendering, tuning physics-based grabbing and locomotion, and crafting spatial audio cues to deepen immersion.
